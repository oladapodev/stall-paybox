# Stall: video script (about 75 seconds)

Talk slowly and smile. Short sentences. It's fine to read from your phone.
Each line you say is in **bold**. What we show on screen is in [brackets].
You only need to film the bold lines. I add everything in [brackets].

---

**1. Hook (0:00 to 0:08)** [your face, big text pops in: "Can AI buy from you?"]

**"More and more people now shop by asking AI. ChatGPT. Claude. But if you sell things, can AI actually buy from you?"**

**2. Problem (0:08 to 0:16)** [your face, then a quick animation: small shops fade to grey]

**"Big stores are ready for this. Small sellers are not. No website, no checkout. So AI just skips them."**

**3. Solution (0:16 to 0:22)** [Stall logo pops in]

**"So I built Stall. A plugin for PayBox that lets anyone open a shop that AI can find, and pay."**

**4. Seller demo (0:22 to 0:36)** [screen: a chat. Seller types "Open a shop called Green Leaf Candles". The AI replies "Your shop is open". Then "Add lavender candle, $12" and the item appears]

**"Here's a seller. She just chats. 'Open my shop. Add a candle, twelve dollars.' Done. No website. She gets a link to share anywhere."**

**5. Buyer demo (0:36 to 0:54)** [screen: a different chat. Buyer types "Find me a scented candle under $15". Results appear. "Buy the lavender one". A PayBox approve card slides up, a finger taps, then "Paid ✅" with a receipt]

**"Now a buyer asks their AI: 'find me a candle under fifteen dollars.' It finds her shop. One tap to approve, and the money goes straight to the seller. No middleman."**

**6. Price drop (0:54 to 1:04)** [screen: "Tell me when it's under $10". Then a bell: "Good news, the price dropped"]

**"You can even say: tell me when it gets cheaper. When the seller drops the price, you're one tap from buying."**

**7. Close (1:04 to 1:15)** [your face, then end card: Stall, Built on PayBox, the GitHub link]

**"Any seller. Anywhere. Shoppable by AI. This is Stall, built on PayBox for the MoonPay hackathon. Thank you!"**

## How to film (5 minutes)

1. Use your phone's **back camera** if someone can hold it. If not, the front camera is fine.
2. Hold the phone **sideways (landscape)**. Prop it at eye level.
3. Face a window so light falls on your face. Avoid a bright window behind you.
4. Quiet room. Phone about an arm's length away, so your head and shoulders are in the shot.
5. Read all 7 parts in **one take**. Pause for 2 seconds between parts. Mistakes are fine, just pause and say the line again. I'll cut them out.
6. Send me the file. Put it in `/home/dev/Desktop/paybox/video/` and tell me the name.
