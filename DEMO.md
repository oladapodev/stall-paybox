# Stall demo script (60-90s)

1. **Hook (5s):** Shop page https://stall-api.oladapo.workers.dev/s/ada-s-fabrics
   "Millions of sellers live on WhatsApp and Instagram. AI agents can't buy from them. Until now."
2. **Seller (15s):** In Claude: "Open a stall called Mama Nkechi's Kitchen, WhatsApp +234..., payout to my Base wallet. Add jollof rice tray $0.80, 5 in stock."
3. **Buyer search (10s):** "Find ankara fabric under $1 on Stall."
4. **Buy (20s):** "Buy the Ankara fabric, deliver to Yaba, Lagos." PayBox approval appears, tap passkey.
5. **Confirm (15s):** "Confirm my order." Show Paid, Basescan receipt, tap the WhatsApp link that opens with the order prefilled.
6. **Price watch (15s):** "Watch for lace under $1." Seller: "Drop French lace to $0.90." Buyer: "Check my watch." Triggered, one tap to buy.
7. **Close (5s):** "Stall. Every WhatsApp seller, shoppable by AI. Built on @PayBox."

# X post

Millions of sellers run their business on WhatsApp and Instagram. AI agents can't shop from any of them.

So I built Stall for the @PayBox hackathon by @moonpay 🛍️

• Sellers open a stall just by chatting
• Buyers tell Claude "find ankara under $20"
• One passkey tap, USDC on Base goes straight to the seller
• Receipt + WhatsApp order message
• Price-drop watches that turn into one-tap buys

No middleman. No custody.
